====================================================
Interactive Slides
====================================================

The slides from class as a web page you can try: drive the clone on any of
the 20 test laps and see where a wheel leaves the lane, change the detector
noise and watch the fitted offset weight shrink, and step through DAgger
iteration by iteration. It runs in your browser, with nothing to install.

.. button-link:: interactive/L11_interactive.html
   :color: primary
   :shadow:

   Open the interactive slides

- The arrow keys or Space go to the next slide; Esc shows the whole lecture
  as a map.
- F goes full screen, T switches between light and dark, and ? lists every
  key.
- Ctrl+K searches the slides by their words. Four "Check" slides ask a
  question from the quiz: click an answer to see whether it is right.
